My Story

Kaylee Thomas

Hi! I'm Kaylee, an Information Technology student at Northern Virginia Community College (NOVA). Alongside my studies, I work in Human Resources, where I've developed a strong interest in how technology can improve communication, organization, and workplace processes. I'm passionate about continuing to learn, building new skills, and exploring opportunities that combine my interests in technology and people.

Throughout my ITE 170 Multimedia course, I've had the opportunity to explore creativity in ways I never expected. From creating my original song, Choosing Us, using Suno AI to designing my Midnight at the Museum poster and video teaser, each project has challenged me to try something new. I've also enjoyed learning how to build websites using HTML and CSS, bringing together my interests in technology, design, and storytelling.

Outside of work and school, I enjoy spending time with family and friends, watching basketball, and supporting the Washington Wizards. I value kindness, personal growth, and staying true to myself. Whether I'm exploring a new creative project or learning something challenging, I enjoy finding opportunities to grow and express my personality.
